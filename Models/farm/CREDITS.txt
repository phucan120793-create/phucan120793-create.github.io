AOTA — farm 3D models
=====================

pig.glb, cow.glb, water_buffalo.glb, chicken.glb, duck.glb, worker.glb
  Generated by the game owner on Meshy AI (Text-to-3D, "Stylized").
  Optimised with gltf-transform (simplify + webp 512 textures).
  Owned by the project — no third-party attribution required.

NOTE: as generated these have NO skeleton / animation yet. They move via
the behaviour system (barnAnimalBehavior.js). Re-export the Meshy
Rig+Animate versions over the same filenames + add anim clip names in
scene/farm/farmModels.js to make them properly animated.
